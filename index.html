How to play & about Pithu

Pithu (also called Lagori or Seven Stones) is a street game played across India, usually at dusk in a courtyard or lane. A stack of flat stones sits in the middle. One side throws a ball to topple it, then scrambles to rebuild the stack while the other side tries to hit them with the ball. Rebuild it before your whole side is out, and your team wins the round.

Move: WASD, arrow keys, or the on-screen joystick. Action: Space, Enter, or the round button — throws the ball (aiming at the stack, or defending) and becomes a quick dodge-roll while your team is rebuilding.

Online multiplayer: up to 6 people can join one room. You're split evenly into Team A and Team B — one team attacks together while the other defends together, then you swap sides each round. First team to 3 round wins takes the match.
