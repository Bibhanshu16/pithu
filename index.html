How to play & about Pithu

Pithu (also called Lagori or Seven Stones) is a street game played across India, usually at dusk in a courtyard or lane. A stack of flat stones sits in the middle. One side throws a ball to topple it, then scrambles to rebuild the stack while the other side tries to hit them with the ball. Rebuild it before you're out, and your side wins the round.

Aiming phase: move to line up with the stack and press ACTION to throw. The defender will try to block you.

Rebuild phase: walk onto scattered stones to pick them up, carry them back to the centre to stack them, and dodge incoming throws. ACTION becomes a quick dodge-roll for the attacker, and a throw for the defender.

P1: WASD to move · Space for action
P2 (multiplayer): Arrow keys to move · Enter for action
