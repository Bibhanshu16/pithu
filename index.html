How to play & Game Rules

Pithu (also called Lagori) is a classic street game. A stack of 7 flat stones sits in the middle. One team throws a ball to topple it, then scrambles to rebuild the stack while the other team tries to hit them with the ball.

Controls: WASD / Arrow Keys or Joystick to move. Space / Action Button: Throws the ball (if defending) or performs a quick dodge-roll (if attacking).

Rules: If you get hit by the ball while attacking, you lose a life. Rebuild all 7 stones before your team runs out of lives to win the round!
